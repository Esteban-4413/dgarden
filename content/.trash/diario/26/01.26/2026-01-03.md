### Compulsorily 
(adv.) In a way that is required by law or a rule 
- example: Every dog that is neutered is compulsorily vaccinated against rabies. 
(British) In a way that involves the officially enforced sale of privately owned land or property for public us 
- example: The council has been given the go-ahead to compulsorily purchase the three remaining flats.
