## Word(s) of the day 

### Skosh 
*(Noun.)*
*slang.* 
1. a bit: a jot 
	example: 
		We need just a skosh more room. 

### Metaphorically 
*(Adverb.)* 
1. In a way that uses or relates to metaphor; figuratively. 
	example: 
		Speaking metaphorically, my dad said I'd just won the lottery 
		he seems, literally and metaphorically, lost.
